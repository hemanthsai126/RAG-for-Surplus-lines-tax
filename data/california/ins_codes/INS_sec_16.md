Insurance Code - INS
GENERAL PROVISIONS
( General Provisions enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145. )
16.
As used in this code the word “shall” is mandatory and the word “may” is permissive, unless otherwise apparent from the context.
(Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
