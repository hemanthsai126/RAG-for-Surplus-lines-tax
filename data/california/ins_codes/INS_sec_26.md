Insurance Code - INS
GENERAL PROVISIONS
( General Provisions enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145. )
26.
“Domestic” means organized under the laws of this State, whether or not admitted.
(Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
