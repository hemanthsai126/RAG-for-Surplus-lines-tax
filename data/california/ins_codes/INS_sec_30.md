Insurance Code - INS
GENERAL PROVISIONS
( General Provisions enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145. )
30.
“Resident” means residing in this State, “nonresident” means not residing in this State.
(Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
